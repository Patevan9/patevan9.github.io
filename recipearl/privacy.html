Recipearl Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Recipearl recipe app for Android ("the app") handles your information. The app is made by Patrick Evan Lippy, Lippy Robotics Labs ("we", "us").

The short version

  • Lippy Robotics Labs has no servers and never receives your recipes. There are no user accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your recipes, photos, notes and shopping list are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage.
  • The app goes online only to read a recipe web page that you choose to save, and its photo. Nothing about you or your recipes is uploaded.
  • Backups are saved only where you choose.

What information the app handles

Everything below is stored in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read.

The app does not ask for or use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photo library, calendar or any advertising ID. Its only permission is internet access, used as described below.

Saving recipes from websites

When you share a recipe link to Recipearl, or paste one in, the app downloads that web page and the recipe's photo so it can read the recipe.

  • The app connects only to the website you chose, using secure HTTPS. Plain web addresses are changed to HTTPS.
  • Like any web browser, this visit lets the website see your internet (IP) address and that a phone browser is visiting. The website handles this under its own privacy policy.
  • If a website does not allow a simple download, the app opens the page invisibly in Android's built-in browser engine, the same one Chrome uses. The website's own scripts can run during this visit, and the website may store cookies, which stay in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • The app only reads the page. It does not send your recipes or any personal information to the website.

Backups and importing files

  • Backup: when you choose Backup, the app saves one file containing all your recipes (including Recently Deleted), their photos and your shopping list. You choose where to save it, for example your phone's storage or a cloud service such as Google Drive. If you save it to a cloud service, that service's privacy policy applies. The backup file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.
  • Restore and import: the app reads only the file you pick, such as a Recipearl backup or a recipe file exported from another recipe app.
  • The app turns off Android's automatic cloud backup, so your recipes are not copied anywhere by the app on its own. Make your own backup before uninstalling the app or changing phones.

Other features that use other apps

  • Search Web: if you choose to look up a word from a recipe, the app opens your phone's web search (for example the Google app or your browser) with that word. That app handles the search under its own privacy terms. The app itself sends nothing.
  • Text tools: when you highlight text, Android can also offer tools from other apps, such as Translate. If you choose one, that app receives the highlighted text under its own privacy terms.
  • Print / PDF: printing uses Android's print service, and the printer or PDF option you choose.
  • Sharing a link to the app uses Android's Share menu.

Third parties

We never receive your recipes or personal information, and we do not sell or rent any information. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software. The only outside parties involved are the ones you choose to use: the recipe websites you save from, the place you save backups to, and the other apps described above.

  • Google Play: when you download or update the app, Google handles that under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Our website, including this page, is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any website host, GitHub handles basic connection details, such as your IP address, to deliver pages, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • Your recipes stay on your phone until you delete them.
  • Deleted recipes go to Recently Deleted first. From there you can restore them, or delete them forever, which also removes their photos.
  • To delete everything the app stored, uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings (Settings → Apps → Recipearl → Storage → Clear storage; the exact names can vary by phone). Backup files you saved are kept until you delete them yourself.
  • Because we never receive your recipes, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you emailed us a problem report or a question, you can ask us to delete that email.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Recipes from other websites

Recipes and photos you save from websites belong to their original authors. Recipearl saves them for your personal use. Please respect the authors' rights when sharing them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. If a change affects how your information is handled, we will update the policy before the app changes.
