Common questions

How do I save a recipe from a website?

In your web browser, open the recipe page, tap Share and choose Recipearl. Or copy the page's web address, open Recipearl, tap Import Recipe and paste it.

A recipe did not save, or something looks wrong.

Some websites do not allow saving, and websites change over time. Please check the saved recipe against the original page and fix anything you need by editing it. If it keeps happening, email us the web address of the recipe page and we will take a look.

How do I back up my recipes?

Open Backup & Restore and tap Create Backup. This saves one file with all your recipes, photos and your shopping list. Choose a safe place for it, such as Google Drive. The file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private.

How do I move my recipes to a new phone?

Make a backup on your old phone first. Then install Recipearl on your new phone, open Backup & Restore, tap Restore from Backup and pick your backup file. Restoring only adds recipes you don't already have, so nothing is replaced.

I deleted a recipe by mistake.

Open Recently Deleted and tap Restore. If you changed a recipe and want the old version back, use Undo Last Edit on that recipe.

Can I bring recipes from another recipe app?

Yes, for supported export files. Use Import from a File and pick the export file. The app lists the file types it supports and shows you what it found before anything is saved.

The app closed unexpectedly.

Sorry about that! Open Recipearl again. If a Problem Report appears, tap Copy Details and paste them into an email to us. The report has no recipes in it, only technical details that help us fix the problem.

Is my information private?

Yes. Recipearl has no account, no ads and no tracking, and your recipes stay on your phone. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
