Features

Everything in one place

Recipearl is built to make collecting and cooking your recipes simple, calm and reliable.

  • 🔗
    Save from websitesFound a recipe online? Share the link to Recipearl and it saves the ingredients, steps and photo for you.
  • 🛡️
    Smart duplicate protectionRecipearl notices when you already have a recipe, lets you compare them, and never overwrites anything.
  • 🗂️
    Organized your wayCategories, photos, your own notes and servings, so every recipe is easy to find.
  • 🛒
    Shopping listAdd a recipe's ingredients to your shopping list and check them off at the store.
  • 🖨️
    Print or save as PDFPrint a recipe for the kitchen, or save it as a PDF to share.
  • 📥
    Bring your recipes alongImport recipes you exported from another recipe app, all in one go.
Peace of mind

Your recipes are safe

Losing a favorite family recipe is no fun. Recipearl is designed so that never happens by accident.

  • ♻️
    Recently DeletedDeleted recipes go to Recently Deleted first, so you can bring them back.
  • ↩️
    Undo Last EditChanged your mind? Go back to how the recipe was before.
  • 💾
    Backup & RestoreSave your whole collection, photos and shopping list to one file, and restore it on a new phone.
Getting started

Saving a recipe is easy

  1. Find a recipe you loveOn any recipe website, in your phone's browser.
  2. Tap Share, then RecipearlRecipearl reads the recipe page and fills in the details.
  3. Check it and saveAdd a category, your notes or servings. It's in your collection, even offline.
Privacy

Your recipes stay with you

🔒No account, no ads, no tracking. Your recipes are kept on your phone. Lippy Robotics Labs has no servers and never receives your recipes. Recipearl only goes online to read a recipe page you choose to save. Read the full privacy policy.
