Captraze

Drive. Capture. Conquer.

A future multiplayer vehicle-combat and territory-control game, designed for mobile. Red versus Blue. Drive in hard, jump out, and hold the pod.

See how you win Where we are
The game

Not just racing. War for territory.

Fast vehicles get you there. Your boots win the fight. CAPTRAZE blends arcade driving with on-foot combat around glowing control pods, and the team that holds the ground takes the match.

01

Drive

Tear across huge arenas in fast, stunt-ready combat vehicles. Ramps, jumps and high-speed routes, with handling built for fun, not realism.

02

Capture

Bail out at the pod and hold the zone. No drive-by steals: you stay, you fight, and only then does the pod turn your color.

03

Conquer

Every pod you own fires a sky-high beam in your team's color. Link your territory, push the front line and break the enemy.

Team Conquest

Control the grid

Red versus Blue. Control pods sit across the map, linked by an energy grid. Take them in order, and you carve a path straight to the enemy's core.

  • Hold to capture. Exit your vehicle and stay inside the pod's zone until it converts.
  • Sky-high beams. Owned pods blaze red or blue, visible from anywhere on the map.
  • Linked nodes. You can only attack a pod that connects to ground you already hold. Proposed
  • Overdrive. Link a full chain to the enemy core and its defenses drop for a final assault. Proposed

Capturing takes real time in the zone. Touching it or driving past does nothing.

Beams don't lie

Look up to read the battle. Beams also use emblems and shapes, not color alone.

Defend or lose it

Push the front

Linked pods create moving battle lines instead of random chaos.

Map concept

Crater Junction

Two fortified bases. One neutral crater in the middle. Three ways to get there, and each one changes the fight.

  • Lane 1: The Main Boulevard. The fast, direct vehicle route through the center.
  • Lane 2: The Outer Overpass. A high ridge with a jump ramp, and a heavy weapon and shield pickup for those who dare.
  • Lane 3: The Low Gully. An on-foot flanking route under the action.

More map ideas, including Industry Forge, are in early exploration.

Arsenal & stunts

Gear up. Then go airborne.

Weapons and supplies are scattered across every map. Walk over them to grab them. Where you place your fights depends on where the good stuff is.

Combat weapons

Primary fire plus a utility mode. Rarer, heavier weapons sit in riskier spots.

Energy shields

Some weapons carry a built-in shield. Limited power, recharge delay. No invincibility.

Throwables

Fictional energy bombs, EMP-style blasts and smoke cover.

Health kits

Patch yourself up on foot and get back in the fight.

Vehicle repair packs

Keep your ride alive, separate from player healing.

Stunts Proposed

Dodge-roll thrusters, jump jets, and high-speed ejections that land you fighting on foot.

Fighters

Nobody here is friendly

Pick an intimidating avatar and fight for either team. Your look is yours. Every fighter plays by the same rules.

The CAPTRAZE code

Fight hard. Play fair.

01

Zero pay-to-win

Money never buys power. Any future purchases are cosmetic: skins, outfits, beam effects and supporter badges.

02

Skill decides

Every avatar plays the same. Stronger pickups are earned on the map, not in a store.

03

Stable before big

A small, solid, fun game first. Bigger matches and features only when they're ready.

04

Built for adults

An 18+ community, with community maps checked and approved before anyone plays them.
